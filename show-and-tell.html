What is the crew working on?

One of the best ways to learn Prog8 is to make something with it! Here are a few projects from our members. Have something to share? Bring it along to Discord. Finished programs and works in progress are both welcome.

XVI2 - a vi-style editor

The Retrodores are working on XVI2, a text editor for the Commander X16 written in Prog8. There is a C64 port too! It has modal editing, file loading and saving, line yank/paste, and horizontal panning for long lines. It is still a work in progress, so come try it out and let us know how it goes.

XVI2 source, builds, and project notes

6502 Fart Bombs

CommodoreSam brings us a Minesweeper-style game with a name that speaks to the 13-year-old game programmer in all of us. Written in Prog8, 6502 Fart Bombs is also a fun look at taking a game across several retro systems. Watch the video to see the ports in action!
